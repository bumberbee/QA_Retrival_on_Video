## Metric audit: every regressed question

Hit@k and MRR score returned **slide-card group start/end**, with any positive half-open overlap (`start < gold_end and end > gold_start`). They do not use the winning chunk time or representative time. Backend chunks are ranked and fused into groups; context may include neighboring chunks. Dev CR checks gold-overlapping **actually retained chunk/word timestamps**, key terms, and final context text. Thus a card can be a hit while its retained OCR is too late, and changing grouping can alter speech partitioning and neighboring context as well as temporal credit.

## Causes and recommendation

**Board:** scoped options expand **18?117 groups** and **73?240 chunks** (OCR 18?117, speech 55?123), while cleaned medium ASR is identical: **5,113 words, zero text differences**. Short states compete for top-3 and reduce useful neighboring speech. Swapping old OCR into the new grouping does not recover the shared board losses. On the identical **488.96 s** frame, old OCR reads `L12 80 70` and `370`; new reads `L12 80 30` and `330`. Restore legacy board grouping and archived old OCR.

**Other OCR/frame changes:** new OCR is beneficial on some videos, so a global rollback loses gains. At **1696.400566 s**, the old chart OCR preserves G, axes and coordinates (including 11/27); new omits those labels and has only a short unverified description. The actual chart was visually checked (`graph-1696.png`). Protect only old `seg_00026` for this exact frame. Chart-video H3 improves old/new/protected **9/11?8/11?10/11**. Editor safe splitting remains useful; compatible unchanged-state representatives improve its H1 old/native-new/old-OCR-swap/compatible **4/11?5/11?6/11?7/11**.
